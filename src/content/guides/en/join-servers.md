## One-click join (easiest)

On this site's [server list](/en/servers), clicking **"Join via CM"** launches Content Manager straight into the server — as long as you have CM installed.

## Find servers in CM

`Content Manager → Online` lets you filter by name / ping and favourite the servers you play most.

## Connect by IP manually

If you know the `IP:port`: on the `Online` page you can add an address manually (top-right) and join.

## Before you join

- Servers often require a **specific CSP version** and a set of **required mods** (cars / tracks) — missing them means you can't join or get kicked.
- CM usually tells you what's missing; just install what it lists.
