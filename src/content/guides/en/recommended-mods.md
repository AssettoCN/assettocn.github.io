These are the **app mods** (tools that live in `apps/`) the community uses most — not cars or tracks. They're all stable, good-looking and quick to get going. For how to install them, see the previous level, **Installing mods**.

> Most of these **require CSP**, and some need a fairly recent version. If one doesn't show up, check your CSP first.

## HUD / interface

### Comfy Map

A clean track map showing you and other cars — especially useful on long cruise maps like Shutoko.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/comfy-map.52623/)

![Comfy Map showing the track layout and car positions](/images/guides/recommended-comfymap.png)

### NFS World Speedometer UI

A Need for Speed: World styled speedometer. Requires **CSP 3.0 or newer**.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/nfs-world-speedometer-ui.80328/)

![NFS World styled speedometer](/images/guides/recommended-nfsworldui.png)

### CMRT Essential HUD

A compact panel that gathers the readouts you check most into one place.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/cmrt-essential-hud.69475/)

![The CMRT Essential HUD panel](/images/guides/recommended-cmrtessentialhud.png)

### Better Essentials

A visual overhaul of the stock essential widgets.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/better-essentials.76651/)

![Better Essentials interface](/images/guides/recommended-betteressentials.png)

## Cameras / screenshots

If you want shots like the ones in the gallery, these are how they're made.

### CamTool2

The long-standing camera tool, with keyframed camera moves.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/camtool-2.15062/)

![CamTool2's camera path editor](/images/guides/recommended-camtool2.png)

### Drone Camera

A free-flying camera — put the shot wherever you want it.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/drone-camera.36405/)

![Drone Camera control panel](/images/guides/recommended-dronecam.png)

### Drone Camera Pro

The advanced version of Drone Camera, with finer control.

[Download](https://www.overtake.gg/downloads/drone-camera-pro.62857/)

![Drone Camera Pro control panel](/images/guides/recommended-dronecampro.png)
