## You can play without a wheel

Xbox controllers are plug-and-play. A **PS4 / PS5 controller** needs [DS4Windows](https://github.com/Ryochan7/DS4Windows/releases) to present it as an Xbox pad (covered back in level 01). A keyboard works in a pinch too.

On a pad, do install a steering assist — stock gamepad steering is hard work in a lot of cars. See Advanced Gamepad Assist and A7 Assist in **Recommended mods**.

## Wheel basics

1. **Install the vendor firmware / driver first.** Search for `brand model wheel firmware` (e.g. "Moza R12 wheel firmware") and set your **steering angle** there.
2. In `Content Manager → Settings → Controls`, select your wheel and assign throttle / brake / clutch / shifting.
3. **Calibrate steering to full lock**, and check that force feedback (FFB) direction is correct and not clipping (overloaded).

> **Logitech users: G HUB must stay open the whole time you play** — closing it drops force feedback.
>
> Also **remap every button in CM after any firmware update** — updates routinely wipe the mapping.

## Force feedback feel

- If FFB is too heavy or clipping, lower the overall gain; if it feels "muddy", add a little detail.
- FFB varies a lot between cars — fine-tune around the car you drive most.
